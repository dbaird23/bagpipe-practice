The bar
Time signature
How many beats fall in a bar, and which note counts as one beat. The 6/8, 9/8 and 12/8 settings count dotted quarter notes, so 6/8 gives two clicks a bar rather than six.
The click
Extra clicks between beats
Quieter clicks in the gaps between the beats, so you can hear where the small notes belong. Leave it off while you are still learning to land on the beat.
Accents
How loud each beat of the bar is. Tap a beat to cycle it through strong, normal, soft and silent. A silent beat still sounds any extra clicks sitting inside it.
Click sound
Wood carries over a practice chanter. Beep carries over a full set of pipes.
Rhythm and lift
Pointing
Snap Pointed
50
How unevenly a pair of notes is played. In the middle both notes are the same length — pipers call that round. To the right the first note gets longer and the second shorter and sharper, the dotted rhythm of a march, which sits around 75. To the left it flips into a short note snapping into a long one, the way a strathspey does.
Beat stretch
None Strong
0
Moves the beats themselves, lengthening the first and shortening the rest to pay for it, so the bar still takes exactly as long as it should. This is not what pipers mean by pulsing a strathspey — that is holding the dotted notes, which is Pointing. A click that moves is no longer something to check your timing against, so leave this at nothing unless you know you want it.
Practice drills
Silent bars
Play bars silent bars
Plays for a few bars, then goes quiet for a few while you keep going. The count carries on underneath, so when it comes back you find out whether your tempo held.
Tempo ramp
Every bars add BPM up to BPM
Raises the tempo a step at a time and stops at the top figure you set. Start a tune slower than you can play it and let it creep up on you.
Playing along
Drone
Level
10
A quiet drone under the click, pitched from the Low A you set for the microphone. Useful for tuning against. The bass note is too low for a phone speaker, so use headphones to hear all of it.
When you cannot hear it

Piobaireachd
There is no piobaireachd setting. The ground of a piobaireachd is not played in strict time, and a click would teach you the wrong thing. Use the plain click for drilling the movements in the variations if you want even fingers.
